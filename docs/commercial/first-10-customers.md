# First 10 Customers

Who ClouDonna should sell to first, and how success and conversion are judged for each. Companion to `first-million-playbook.md` (overall sales model) and `decision-sprint-delivery-playbook.md` (delivery). No founder personal details appear here.

## Ideal customer profile

Mid-market and enterprise organizations facing one major Data, AI, Cloud, SAP, Analytics, Architecture, or Technology platform decision — the same ICP as the broader commercial model (`first-million-playbook.md`), narrowed here to what makes a *first* customer specifically good: a real, bounded decision that can complete a Decision Sprint in 2-4 weeks without depending on unbuilt capability.

## Priority use cases for the first 10

- A platform migration or consolidation decision with 2-4 genuinely distinct vendor options already identified
- A decision where a business case is explicitly required before it can proceed (the Business Case Engine is ClouDonna's strongest, most complete capability)
- A decision where vendor-neutrality concerns are already present — the easiest "why ClouDonna and not a vendor-affiliated advisor" conversation

Avoid, for the first 10 specifically: multi-decision transformation programs (Strategic Decision Program territory — save for later once Decision Sprint delivery is proven), and decisions requiring enterprise governance features (RBAC, organization-private evidence at scale) before Enterprise has a live reference customer of its own.

## Qualification criteria

Same bar as every Decision Sprint qualification (`first-million-playbook.md`): real and specific decision, at least two distinct options, a real decision-maker engaged, timeframe within the quarter or year. For the first 10 specifically, also weight:

- Willingness to be a reference, even privately (an early, honest "would you let us ask again later" conversation — never a condition of the deal)
- A decision-maker who will personally sit through the Week 4 executive review — the Decision Sprint's value is proven or disproven there

## Commercial package

Decision Sprint only, for the first 10. Team and Enterprise expansion should be earned from a completed Sprint's own outcome (the conversion-trigger signals in `first-million-playbook.md`), not sold up front before ClouDonna has a single delivered outcome to point to.

## Success criteria per customer

- A completed Decision Sprint with a recorded outcome, whichever way the decision went
- A Decision Record saved to Decision Memory
- At least one assumption marked CONFIRMED, INVALIDATED, or PARTIALLY_TRUE after the fact, not left UNKNOWN indefinitely
- Customer reference permission discussed, whatever the answer

## Conversion strategy

Each completed Sprint is reviewed against the real Team-expansion and Enterprise-expansion signals (`expansion-signals.ts`, surfaced on the account page) before any follow-up pitch — never a scripted upsell independent of what the engagement actually revealed.

## Reference strategy

Default is PRIVATE, no public use, for every one of the first 10 (`commercial_customer_references`). Only move to ANONYMOUS_ALLOWED / LOGO_ALLOWED / CASE_STUDY_ALLOWED on the customer's own explicit, recorded consent. The first public case study should come from whichever of the 10 has the strongest outcome and the clearest consent — not necessarily the first one signed.

## Outcome capture

Every one of the first 10 is disproportionately valuable as learning data (`docs/capabilities/revenue-operations.md`'s customer outcome learning foundation) — assumption validation and business case actuals from these 10 engagements directly calibrate how confident ClouDonna can be in its own business case numbers for customer 11 onward.
