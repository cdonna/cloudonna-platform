# Decision Sprint Delivery Playbook

How the ClouDonna team runs a Decision Sprint engagement, week by week. A planning structure, not a fixed promise — real engagements compress or extend depending on the decision's complexity and the customer's own pace. Never promise an exact timeline publicly; this structure is internal delivery guidance, not a contract term.

## Week 0 — Scope

- Confirm the decision, the options genuinely on the table, and the decision-maker
- Agree scope, start date, and target completion date; record in `decision_sprints`
- Identify the executive sponsor and the working team on both sides
- Resolve open security questions before committing a start date
- Check delivery capacity (the documented concurrent-sprint assumption) before confirming the date

## Week 1 — Context and evidence

- Capture enterprise context: constraints, existing platforms, regulatory factors
- Create the Decision Workspace
- Begin evidence collection via Research Agent and Vendor Intelligence
- Record the customer's own decision deadline and investment at stake, if not already captured during qualification

## Week 2 — Analysis and economics

- Build the business case: deterministic TCO/ROI/NPV/payback per option
- Run economic scenario analysis and sensitivity
- Resolve any conflicting or stale evidence surfaced by the Evidence Graph

## Week 3 — Challenge and scenarios

- Run Decision Challenger against the leading recommendation
- Stress-test the business case's largest assumptions and uncertainties
- Prepare the Executive Recommendation across strategic, architecture, and economic lenses

## Week 4 — Executive decision

- Hold the executive review with the sponsor
- Record the final human decision — accepting the recommendation and choosing differently are both valid, recorded outcomes
- Save the Decision Record to Decision Memory
- Capture the Decision Sprint outcome (recommendation, decision, reasons, next step, expansion potential)
- Open the expansion review

## Shorter engagements

A narrower decision with fewer options or an already-complete business case can compress Weeks 1-3 — the Sprint's own `scope` field is what actually drives the engagement, not this template. Never compress Week 4 (executive review and outcome capture); that is where the Decision Record and the proprietary learning data this engine depends on actually get created.

## After the Sprint

Every outcome feeds `docs/capabilities/revenue-operations.md`'s customer outcome learning foundation — tenant-private, never exposed outside the customer's own organization, never copied into global Vendor Intelligence automatically. This is what makes the second Decision Sprint, for any customer, faster than the first.
