## ClouDonna

### Enterprise Decision Intelligence

**Evidence-backed. Economically defensible. Vendor-neutral.**

Enterprise technology decisions — which data platform, which cloud, which AI stack to commit to — routinely carry more budget and more risk than almost any other decision a company makes, and are too often made on a vendor pitch deck, a handful of reference calls and a gut feeling.

ClouDonna exists to bring the same rigor to these decisions that enterprises already expect from their financial ones: a structured case, explicit evidence, a defensible recommendation, and a durable record of what was actually decided and why.

## What Enterprise Decision Intelligence means, in practice

Four capabilities working together, not one:

**Decision Intelligence** — a deterministic scoring engine that turns requirements into a ranked, explainable recommendation across ten scored dimensions (architecture, business fit, technology, governance, AI readiness, security, ecosystem, cost, time-to-value, strategic fit). The same input always produces the same score; AI never sets a score directly.

**Evidence Intelligence** — live research that gathers, classifies and attaches provenance to the evidence behind a recommendation. Every claim carries one of six evidence types (official source, customer input, observed data, calculated, AI interpretation, assumption) — a claim is never evidence just because it sounds confident.

**Business Case Intelligence** — a deterministic Business Case Engine: TCO, ROI, NPV and payback across 1/3/5-year horizons, economic scenarios, financial sensitivity, and a Cost of Delay model. Every financial figure is either an input, an explicit assumption, or a calculated output — never an AI-set number.

**Scenario Intelligence** — the same decision stress-tested against changed assumptions, costs and constraints, plus a Decision Challenger that actively looks for the weakest assumption, the most fragile part of the recommendation, and what would flip it. The Challenger can propose a challenge; it cannot change a score.

**Decision Memory** — every decision kept as a durable, versioned record — what was decided, what was known at the time, and what has changed since — instead of a slide nobody can find again.

## Why vendor-neutral matters

ClouDonna sells no implementation services and takes no vendor placement fees. Neutrality is enforced structurally — no vendor-name branching in the scoring engine, regression-tested — not just promised.
