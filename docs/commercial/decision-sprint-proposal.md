# Decision Sprint Proposal Template

**Status:** Structural template for a real Decision Sprint proposal (brief §28). Fill in every bracketed placeholder with the actual customer's content — nothing below is legal contract language; commercial terms beyond price and payment timing belong in a separate, counsel-reviewed agreement, not this document.

---

## [Customer Name] — Decision Sprint Proposal

### 1. Decision

**The decision:** [One sentence — e.g. "Which platform should [Customer] standardize on for its enterprise data and AI estate: SAP Business Data Cloud, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, or a hybrid architecture."]

**Why now:** [The trigger — budget cycle, contract renewal, regulatory deadline, transformation program kickoff.]

**Decision Value:** [The customer's own estimate of what is economically at stake — e.g. "Approximately CHF [X]M in platform, migration and multi-year run costs." Never a ClouDonna estimate of the customer's business; always their own figure, captured at qualification and confirmed here.]

### 2. Business context

[2–4 sentences: current landscape, what's driving the need for a decision, who is involved, what has already been tried or considered.]

### 3. Scope

**Options in scope:** [List the specific options — e.g. "SAP BDC, Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, Microsoft Fabric, and a hybrid SAP+Databricks architecture."]

**Out of scope:** [Anything explicitly excluded, to avoid later scope disagreement.]

### 4. Outputs

Everything below is a real, live ClouDonna capability — nothing promised here is a roadmap feature:

- Structured decision context, options and constraints
- Evidence and Vendor Intelligence, with provenance (source, confidence, freshness)
- Live research against the real options under consideration
- A deterministic Business Case: TCO, ROI, NPV and payback across a 1/3/5-year horizon
- Economic scenarios (base/upside/downside) and financial sensitivity
- A Decision Challenger review — the weakest assumption, the most fragile part of the recommendation, what would flip it
- An Executive Recommendation across strategic, architecture and economic lenses, with any disagreement between them stated explicitly rather than hidden
- A saved Decision Record in Decision Memory
- An executive brief

### 5. Approach

[Typically: kickoff and decision framing → evidence and research → business case construction → scenario and sensitivity analysis → Challenger review → executive recommendation and brief → review session with stakeholders.]

### 6. Timeline

**Duration:** [2–4 weeks, scoped to actual complexity.]

**Key dates:** [Kickoff date, interim checkpoint if applicable, final readout date.]

### 7. Commercials

**Price:** CHF [amount], starting from CHF 25,000 for the baseline scope described above.

**Payment:** [e.g. "50% on kickoff, 50% on delivery of the executive brief" — or whatever ClouDonna's actual standard terms are; this template does not prescribe them.]

**What changes the price:** [Number of options beyond the baseline, volume of existing documentation to review, number of stakeholder workshops.]

### 8. Customer responsibilities

- Name a single decision owner as the primary point of contact
- Make relevant existing documentation available (current architecture, prior vendor conversations, budget constraints)
- Make the approving stakeholder(s) available for at least one review session
- Confirm the Decision Value estimate and investment context

### 9. ClouDonna responsibilities

- Deliver the outputs listed in Section 4 within the agreed timeline
- Keep all vendor evaluation strictly neutral — no vendor pays for placement or a better outcome
- Keep the customer's own evidence and context private to their organization
- Provide the final Decision Record in a form the customer can revisit independently afterward

### 10. Next steps

1. [Customer] confirms scope and commercials.
2. ClouDonna schedules kickoff.
3. Decision Sprint begins.
