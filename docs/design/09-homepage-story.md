# The Homepage Story

Not sections. Chapters. A visitor should feel like they scrolled through an argument being made *to* them, in order, each chapter earning the next — not a page they skimmed for the parts that applied to them.

## Chapter I — Opening Scene

Full-bleed Deep Space. Silence, visually — no headline yet, no navigation chrome competing for the first half-second. Aurora drifts, barely perceptibly, in the void. This chapter's entire job is a single feeling before a single word is read: *this is not another SaaS product loading.*

## Chapter II — Hero

The headline arrives on Obsidian, `text-cinematic` scale, one sentence, goal-first (unchanged copy instinct from the existing homepage plan — the words were never the problem). Beneath it, one line of supporting text, no more. One CTA, Aurora-lit rather than gradient-filled (`05-color-system.md`). This is the only chapter with a call to action until the final one — every chapter between here and there argues, it doesn't ask.

## Chapter III — Problem

The register shifts, not the darkness — still Obsidian, but the ambient motion stills. A single, specific sentence names the actual problem (undocumented, unaccountable, unevidenced decisions) without naming ClouDonna yet. No illustration. If a sentence needs an illustration to land, the sentence isn't sharp enough yet.

## Chapter IV — Transformation

The chain (`Business Goal → Capability → ... → Decision`) begins tracing in, pinned to scroll position, each node arriving as the reader's own scroll passes it — the reader controls the pace of the transformation, which is the entire point: they are being shown a *process*, not told a claim. This is the chapter that earns the cinematic budget more than any other.

## Chapter V — Decision Intelligence

The page recomposes from Obsidian to the Calm register's light surface — the single deliberate rhythm-break named in `07-layout-system.md`. This is where the product itself is finally shown: a real Decision Score, real evidence, real confidence decomposition, rendered exactly as it appears inside the application. No screenshot trickery, no idealized mockup — the actual UI, because a claim of honesty that uses a fake screenshot to make its case has already undermined itself.

## Chapter VI — Trust

Still light, still calm. Vendor neutrality, the deterministic engine, immutable history — each stated as a specific, checkable fact, not a value adjective. This chapter is written the way `docs/constitution/06-definition-of-trust.md` insists trust actually works: nothing here is asked to be believed on tone alone.

## Chapter VII — Vision

A brief return toward Obsidian, not full Deep Space — the category ambition (`docs/company/02-category-definition.md`), stated once, plainly, without the earlier chapters' cinematic weight repeated. This chapter is short on purpose: having earned trust in Chapter VI, the ambition doesn't need to be sold hard, only stated.

## Chapter VIII — Final CTA

Aurora returns, one glow, one action — structurally a mirror of Chapter II, so the visitor recognizes they've arrived back where they started, now with an argument behind them instead of ahead of them. The single CTA from Chapter II, repeated verbatim, never a second, different offer introduced late in the page.

## The rule governing every chapter

Each chapter has exactly one job and stops the moment that job is done — no chapter is allowed to also carry a secondary sales point "while it has the reader's attention." A story that keeps circling back to sell harder isn't confident in its own argument; this one is built to need exactly one pass.
