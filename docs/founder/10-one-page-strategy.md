# ClouDonna, in One Page — for a Fortune 500 CIO

**The problem.** Your organization makes multi-million-dollar technology platform decisions with less rigor than it applies to far smaller capital expenditures. The evidence behind the last one is scattered across demo notes and Slack threads. The reasoning behind the one before that left with whoever made the call. When your board or your successor asks "why did we choose this," the honest answer is usually reconstructed after the fact, not retrieved.

**What ClouDonna is.** A decision system, not a chatbot and not a comparison site. Every recommendation comes from one deterministic, reproducible scoring engine — not from an AI's best guess. An AI layer explains the reasoning in plain language, but it never sets the score; that boundary is structural, not a policy we promise to honor. Every recommendation is evidenced, versioned permanently, and rebuildable months later exactly as it was when you made the call.

**Why we're neutral, provably, not just by claim.** Our scoring engine has no field a vendor relationship could occupy. We don't take vendor placement fees. We're not selling implementation hours, so we have no incentive to steer you toward whatever we could staff. This isn't a policy on a page — it's a property of the code, checkable by your own security team.

**What you get.** A permanent, defensible record for every major platform decision your team makes — the evidence, the reasoning, the confidence, and, over time, whether it actually worked. Something you can hand to an audit committee. Something your successor can actually understand three years from now.

**Where we are today, honestly.** We are early. The unauthenticated evaluation experience is live and real. Persistent decision history, versioning, and replay are built and moving toward production. Our knowledge base for the first decision category we support is being built out from illustrative to fully verified, deliberately, in the open. We will tell you exactly which parts of this pitch are live today and which are still roadmap — every time, without exception, because that's the same discipline the evidence in your recommendations will carry.

**What we're asking.** Not a purchase order. A real conversation about a real decision you're facing right now, and your honest reaction to what we show you. If it's not useful, we want to know exactly why.

**Why now.** AI has made confident-sounding advice cheap and abundant, and correspondingly harder to trust. The market needs a credible, neutral, evidenced counterweight more than it did three years ago — that gap is real, it's widening, and nobody currently occupies it fully. We intend to.
